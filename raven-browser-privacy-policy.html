Raven Browser Privacy Policy Raven Browser is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data, device permissions, and third-party services when you use Raven Browser. 1. NO PERSONAL DATA COLLECTION We do not collect, store, sell, or monetize any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts). Raven Browser does not require account registration or login. We do not operate remote servers that monitor or record your browsing history, search queries, or passwords. 2. LOCAL ON-DEVICE STORAGE - History & Bookmarks: Browsing history and bookmarks are saved strictly on your local device storage. - Cookies & Cache: Stored locally by the web component to speed up page loading and maintain website login sessions. - User Control: You have complete control to delete your browsing history, cookies, and cache at any time via Settings or automatically upon exit using the "Exit & Clear" feature. 3. ADVANCED PRIVACY & SECURITY FEATURES - Incognito Mode: Does not save history, cookies, or cache; blocks geolocation; spoofs browser navigator identity; and prevents WebRTC IP leaks. - AdBlocker & Anti-Tracking: Blocks analytics scripts, tracking beacons, and telemetry on web pages to protect your privacy. - DNS over HTTPS (DoH): Supports encrypted DNS queries (Cloudflare, Quad9, Google, AdGuard) to prevent network snooping or ISP eavesdropping. - Integrated Proxy/VPN: Provides secure routing options to protect your network traffic. 4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS & USAGE - Internet & Network State: Required to browse web pages, download files, and load advertisements. - Storage & Media Access: Strictly used to save downloaded files to your device storage and play your downloaded media in built-in players. We never access, scan, or upload your private personal files. - Foreground Services: Used to keep audio/video playback active in the background, ensure reliable multi-threaded downloads, and monitor proxy/VPN status. - Notifications: Displays media playback controls in the status bar, download progress indicators, and VPN status. - Display Over Other Apps: Used exclusively for floating video Picture-in-Picture (PiP) window upon user request. - Home Screen Shortcuts: Used to create quick website shortcuts on your home screen at your request. 5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & ADVERTISING Raven Browser integrates reputable third-party advertising networks to support free app maintenance: - Google AdMob - Unity Ads (via Google Mobile Ads mediation) These ad partners may collect and process anonymous technical data (such as Google Advertising ID/GAID, coarse IP location, device model, and ad interaction metrics) to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud. You can opt out or reset your advertising identifier anytime in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). 6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY Raven Browser is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. 7. THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES As a web browser, Raven Browser allows you to visit external websites across the internet. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites you choose to visit. 8. POLICY UPDATES We may update this privacy policy from time to time as new features are introduced. By continuing to use Raven Browser, you acknowledge and agree to this policy.
